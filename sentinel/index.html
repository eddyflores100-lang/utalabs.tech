A malicious MCP server doesn't announce itself in the README. It hides behind obfuscated imports, computed import() paths, and cleanup functions that quietly wipe your .ssh folder. Sentinel is the engine that doesn't blink: 10 static layers over the source, then an L2 sandbox that actually runs the server under gVisor and records every syscall for 30 seconds.
