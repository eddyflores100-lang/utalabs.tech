RULES

House rules

Four rules govern every file in this cabinet. They are the difference between security theater and security.

fail-closed

If the authority can't answer, the spend doesn't happen. Unreachable revocation check → DENY. Expired card → DENY. Downtime degrades to “no”, never to “probably fine”. Implemented in UTA's verifier and in uta-shield's gates.

receipts, not vibes

Every decision — including every refusal — returns a signed receipt with a machine-readable reason and the identity of the authority that answered. Auditors read why, not just what.

stranger-test

Trust that requires membership is not trust — it's a guest list. Every claim on this site is re-derivable by a stranger from live public URLs, in ~30 seconds, with no account and no reliance on our endpoints. Try the Rekor verifier yourself.

honesty policy

solid line = shipped. dashed line = design hook. READMEs and this site mark exactly what ships today — no inflated claims, no stealth betas presented as products.

Q&A

Fair questions

Is UTA the same thing as ATC?+
No. ATC is the protocol — a signed card format with a spec, a lifecycle and a revocation registry. UTA is the adapter that speaks every format (ATC among them) and translates/verifies through one canonical schema. Think passport vs. border checkpoint.
Why so many products? Is this one product?+
It's one question with four answers, at four layers: ATC says who the agent is, UTA understands any credential it shows, Sentinel audits what its tools actually do, Interceptor decides at runtime. uta-shield and MarketNow deploy the same stack on the operator and registry sides.
Can I use this today, for free?+
Yes, in several ways: the x402 verification API is pay-per-call with no signup ($0.01 on Base, free on Sepolia), uta-shield is free to self-host, the MarketNow MCP API is free, and ATC spec + conformance vectors are public. Commercial licensing (white-label, OEM) exists for the managed tiers.
Who is behind this?+
AliceLabs LLC (Wyoming, USA) — Edison Flores & Alejandro Flores. The stack is built in the open: solana-foundation/pay PR #525, x402 wg-identity #40, and a public postmortem culture (see the 2026-09-08 CA rotation incident report).
