© 2026 AliceLabs LLC. All products in this cabinet — UTA, ATC, Sentinel, Interceptor, uta-shield, MarketNow — are AliceLabs LLC products. This page states what we collect (almost nothing), what you may do (a lot), and what you may not (the usual).

§1Privacy policy

This website collects nothing. utalabs.tech is a static site: no cookies, no analytics, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers, no ads. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as any HTTP request does — that is the entire extent of passive data flow on this domain.

Our products follow the same instinct. The stack's standing rule is no silent telemetry: UTA's verifier returns signed receipts and logs nothing it doesn't have to; uta-shield's audit trail records indicators and decisions — never raw arguments — and stays on your infrastructure, not ours; Sentinel audits run inside sandboxes with no real network and no real filesystem.

Paid API calls (x402) are settled on-chain (USDC on Base). Payment transactions are public by nature of the blockchain; we do not attach identity to them beyond what the protocol itself requires. Verification requests may retain: the credential hash, the verdict, the timestamp and the authority that answered — sufficient for the receipt, nothing more. Revocation data is public by design (MNR-CRL-1.0 + transparency log).

Contact data. If you email us, we keep the email — that's how email works. We don't sell, rent or share contact data. There is no marketing list to opt out of.

§2Terms of service

The site. utalabs.tech is provided as-is, for information about AliceLabs LLC products. We document what ships and what doesn't; still, software is software — no warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose or uninterrupted availability. Don't make spending decisions for humans based on this marketing site alone; read the specs and the code.

The fail-closed caveat, in legalese. Our products are designed to refuse operations when verification is impossible. This means our (or any) outage can cause legitimate calls to be denied. That behavior is a feature, is documented, and is not a defect. If your workload cannot tolerate a denial, configure a human-approval fallback — don't ask us to weaken the default.

APIs. Free endpoints (MarketNow MCP tools, ATC verification) are offered in good faith and may be rate-limited, changed or discontinued with notice on the status page. Paid x402 endpoints are paid per call — no subscription, no account, no surprises on an invoice. Whatever settles on-chain is settled.

Liability. To the maximum extent permitted by law, AliceLabs LLC is not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from use of the site or the products. Security products reduce risk; nothing reduces it to zero. You remain responsible for your own deployment, keys and policies.

§3Acceptable use

You may use the stack to make agents more trustworthy. You may not use it to make misbehavior more believable. Concretely, the following are prohibited on all AliceLabs services:

  • issuing or attempting to issue Agent Trust Cards for agents you do not control, or falsifying capability declarations;
  • using verification or audit verdicts to defraud third parties (e.g., presenting a PASS as an endorsement of overall safety);
  • attacking the infrastructure: load abuse of free APIs, sandbox escape attempts, revocation-registry forgery;
  • submitting skills to the marketplace containing malware, credential stealers or undisclosed exfiltration — Sentinel will catch them, quarantine is public, and that's on you;
  • any use violating applicable law, including sanctions and export-control regimes.

Reports of abuse go to the contact below and are handled like our incident reports: documented, answered, and — when confirmed — enforced. We publish what we can.
