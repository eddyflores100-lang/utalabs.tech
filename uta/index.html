File 01 — make every credential speak every dialect

Every agent trust format, one socket.

Eight ecosystems issued eight credential formats. Every verifier that wants to check an agent must speak all of them — or trust only one. UTA is the adapter in between: 8 formats in, one canonical schema out, losslessly — then a 12-stage pipeline decides. If the answer can't be verified, the verdict is DENY. Never “probably fine”.

RULE. 01

The fail-closed clause

The clause: if the revocation check cannot be reached, the verdict is DENY. Not “allow with a warning”. Not “retry silently”. A verifier that can't check is a verifier that doesn't answer — and an unanswered checkpoint is a closed one.
Why you should care: most “trust layers” on the market fail open — when their API is down, calls flow. That inverts the incentive: your security is the first thing sacrificed to their uptime metrics. Ours inverts it back.
